About

I previously led the development of algorithms for AI agents at Sandwich Lab and AgiBot.

My research interests lie in how agents plan, act, and learn from experience. During my work on embodied and digital agents, I realized that pretraining can never fully anticipate what real-world tasks will require, so agents need continual learning infrastructure to keep evolving as they work.

I’m now exploring how people might work with these evolving agents in an era of superintelligence and what kinds of products could emerge from that collaboration.
